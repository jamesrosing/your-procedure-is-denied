# The video: the letter, the case, the model at work, the numbers, the instrument

Two minutes fifty-five seconds, one screen recording of <https://demo.hemeehr.com/> at 1920 by 1080, one voice track, no second clip. The script is the author's own words, his fourth, revised by him on the evening of Nov 2 after the gate reached v1.7 (agreement 44 to 50, 12,242 corruptions), written after a reviewer's note on the first cut (open on the denial, zoom on what is being spoken about, put the headline numbers on screen, scroll the letter, end under the limit) and after Mark Cuban's filter for healthcare AI, that it nets to zero when both sides can buy the same model (README 4.5): the script now says where the asymmetry is, the record one side holds and a gate either side can run. The picture is driven by a script that presses each control at its beat, calls the presenter spotlight (`window.ypid.spotlight`, build/viewer/src/spotlight.ts) at the word it belongs to, zooms the page about the panel being spoken about, and draws the captions; after the voice is recorded, every one of those times is re-set to the recorded words, so the picture follows the voice and not the plan. 449 spoken words; at 140 words a minute that is 192 seconds, so the author's own slot times below assume a quicker read in the opening two beats (0:00 64 words in 24 s, 0:24 119 words in 36 s, 1:15 38 words in 15 s, 1:30 47 words in 15 s, 1:57 57 words in 18 s). `narration.srt` carries the same words as 33 caption cues on the planned times. The take is recorded in the dark theme and in the light; the author picks one by contrast on a phone.

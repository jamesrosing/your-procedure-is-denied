# The video: the letter, the case, the model at work, the numbers, the instrument

Two minutes fifty-five seconds, one screen recording of <https://demo.hemeehr.com/> at 1920 by 1080, one voice track, no second clip. The script is the author's own words, his fourth (Nov 2), written after a reviewer's note on the first cut (open on the denial, zoom on what is being spoken about, put the headline numbers on screen, scroll the letter, end under the limit) and after Mark Cuban's filter for healthcare AI, that it nets to zero when both sides can buy the same model (README 4.5): the script now says where the asymmetry is, the record one side holds and a gate either side can run. The picture is driven by a script that presses each control at its beat, calls the presenter spotlight (`window.ypid.spotlight`, build/viewer/src/spotlight.ts) at the word it belongs to, zooms the page about the panel being spoken about, and draws the captions; after the voice is recorded, every one of those times is re-set to the recorded words, so the picture follows the voice and not the plan. 385 spoken words; at 140 words a minute that is 165 seconds, so the author's own slot times below assume a quicker read in the opening two beats (0:24 87 words in 36 s, 1:15 39 words in 15 s, 1:30 41 words in 15 s, 1:57 44 words in 18 s). `narration.srt` carries the same words as 32 caption cues on the planned times. The take is recorded in the dark theme and in the light; the author picks one by contrast on a phone.

The featured case is case-025: intensity modulated radiation therapy planning after lumpectomy for breast cancer, a 47-year-old woman, denied by a Medicare Advantage plan as not medically necessary, $25,000 at stake; the muse-spark-1.3 live-CHART run read 6 of 27 entries, verified all 8 sentences, took 12.8 seconds and cost $0.024. ## The cut

- One browser take, 2:55, recorded headlessly from a local build of the viewer; no terminal clip in this version (the reviewer's note: the least visual twelve seconds of the first cut).
- The voice is recorded against the planned picture, then transcribed to word times, and the picture is recorded again to those times (`voice-timing.json`), the voice normalized and muxed, the captions burned in from the recorded transcript. Judges watch with the sound off more often than not.

## Every number spoken, and its source

- Nets to zero when both sides can buy the same model; the payer holds the policy, the physician holds the record; a gate either side can run: README 4.5 and its Cuban footnote; the gate as a published standard, README 1.3 and 2.8.
- A second to deny, hours to appeal, most never appealed: README 1.1 (Cigna PxDx 1.2 s per denial; AMA 13 h/week; KFF 11.7 percent of in-network denials appealed).
- Fifty synthetic cases, no real patient anywhere: build/synthetic/README.md and README 2.3; the Ethics declaration. The letter on screen is build/synthetic/letters/case-025.png, generated from the synthetic case (README 2.11).
- The 47-year-old, IMRT after lumpectomy, Medicare Advantage: build/synthetic/cases/case-025.json (patient, procedure, payer, denial CARC 50).
- The coverage index query: the `policy_status` call in the case-025 trace asks CHART for authority cms, code 77301, date of service 2026-09-02, and is answered "criteria last changed 2026-09-27, last checked 2026-09-28". It returns when the criteria last changed and were last checked, not a policy version or text (README 2.5), so the accurate spoken line is that the model asks when this plan's policy last changed, not which version was in force.
- The entries opened, eight of eight, twelve seconds, two cents: the case-025 result in the muse-spark-1.3 live-CHART run (`reading.entriesRead` 6 of 27, `gate.verdict` pass, `latencyMs` 12816, `costUsd` 0.0239).
- The gate's checks (quote, number, date, code, side), not a model: README 2.4, build/src/litmus.ts v1.5.
- 6.1 to 8.1 Gy refuted in the browser: build/viewer/src/gatecheck.ts over case-025 sentence 4; 8.1 is in no admissible source.

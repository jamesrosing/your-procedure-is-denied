# Code of conduct

This project discusses decisions that have harmed people. Patients, physicians, billers, and people who work at insurers all take part here, and some of them will disagree sharply. The discussion stays useful only if it stays civil and stays on the evidence.

## Expectations

Argue the claim, not the person. Critique of the README, the standard, or the software is welcome and expected. Personal attacks, harassment, and discriminatory remarks are not.

Describe allegations as allegations. Pending litigation and investigations are described here with care, and the insurers' disputes of the characterizations are noted. Keep that discipline in issues and pull requests.

Never post real patient data. This includes your own medical details beyond what you choose to share, and anyone else's in any form. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Do not name or identify individual employees of payers, reviewers, or other private individuals. Companies and public filings are fair subjects; people doing their jobs are not.

Assume the other participant wants patients to get appropriate care. Most do.

## Enforcement

Comments or contributions that violate these expectations will be edited or removed, and repeat or serious violations will result in being blocked from the repository. Decisions are made by the maintainer, James H. Rosing.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through GitHub, or use GitHub's own reporting tools for abuse. Reports are handled as confidentially as the platform allows.

## Scope

This applies to all spaces of this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, and commits.
